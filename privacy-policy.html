Thanks you for using CLYP ("the App") . This Privacy Policy explains how we handle user data.

The App is designed with privacy as a top priority. All image processing is performed locally on the user’s device.

Advertising and Data Collection

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

The App uses a third-party advertising service, Google AdMob, to display ads. AdMob may collect non-personally identifiable information for advertising purposes, including:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS and ADID on Android)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, etc.)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, etc.)

This information is managed by Google and handled according to Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Processing and Storage

All image processing features, including background removal and collage generation, are performed entirely on the user’s device.
The developer does not access or collect any user-generated content such as photos or text.

Except for anonymized advertising data described above, no user data is transmitted to external servers.

When the App is deleted, all locally stored data is removed from the device.

Permissions

The App requests the following permissions:

  • Photo Library: Used to load and save images

In-App Purchases

The App offers Pro features through Apple’s In-App Purchase system.

All payments are processed by the Apple App Store. The developer does not store or have access to payment information such as credit card details.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
